Chocks Away! Jet Aces - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Chocks Away! Jet Aces is made by We Make Games Ltd.

The game does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. It has no adverts, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not need an internet connection to play (only buying or restoring the full game goes through Google Play).

If you buy the full game, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play, under Google's own privacy policy. We never see your name, your payment details or your Google account; the game only asks Google Play whether the full game has been bought on this account.

Your progress (missions unlocked, stars, scores, medals, the logbook and darts records) and your settings (sound, music, volume, vibration and controls) are saved only on your own device. They are deleted if you uninstall the game, and you can also wipe your progress at any time from Options > Reset Progress.

The game uses the phone's vibration only if you leave the Vibration option switched on.

Music by Alex Morgan, and by alanajordan, nickpanek, mcanden and music_for_videos on Pixabay.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
